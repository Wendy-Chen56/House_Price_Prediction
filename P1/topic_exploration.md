# Topic Exploration

## Real-World Applications of Analytics

Before choosing my final project, I looked at different ways businesses could use data analytics to solve problems.

**1. Supervised Machine Learning**

A real estate company can use past house sales to estimate the price of a property. This can help real estate agents and sellers make better pricing decisions.

**2. Unsupervised Machine Learning**

A retail company can group customers based on their shopping habits. The company can use these groups to offer more relevant promotions instead of sending the same offers to everyone.

**3. Dimensionality Reduction**

A company may collect many customer variables, but not all of them provide useful information. Dimensionality reduction can help simplify the data and make patterns easier to understand.

**4. Natural Language Processing (NLP)**

An online retailer can analyze customer reviews to identify common complaints, such as shipping delays or product quality issues. The company can use the results to decide which problems to address first.

**5. Sentiment Analysis**

A hotel can analyze online reviews to understand whether guests are satisfied or dissatisfied. Managers can use this information to identify areas that need improvement.

**6. Social Media Analytics**

A business can examine engagement with its social media posts to understand what content attracts customers. This information can help the marketing team plan future campaigns.

## Selected Project: House Price Prediction

I chose supervised machine learning because I wanted to work on a problem that could support a real business decision.

My project uses historical housing data from Ames, Iowa, to predict house sale prices.

The main stakeholders are real estate agents and home sellers. They could use the predicted prices as an additional reference when deciding how much to list a property for.

I compared Linear Regression, Ridge Regression, Huber Regression, and Random Forest to understand which model works better with this dataset.

The goal is not to replace professional property appraisals. Instead, the model could provide another useful reference when making pricing decisions.

The dataset comes from the Kaggle House Prices – Advanced Regression Techniques competition.

Dataset: https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/house-prices-advanced-regression-techniques/overview
